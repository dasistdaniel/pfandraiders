Files: m01.png .. m12.png (Males/M_01..M_12), f01.png .. f12.png (Females/F_01..F_12),
renamed only, pixels unchanged. Each sheet is 64x51 px = 4 columns x 3 rows of 16x17 px frames:
column = direction (0 front/down, 1 side facing right, 2 back/up, 3 side facing left),
row = walk frame (0 standing, 1 step, 2 other step).
